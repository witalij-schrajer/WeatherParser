## 3. Verified source facts

### 3.1 Product descriptions

All four descriptions are version v24.03 (publication date 2024-03-29, last edited 2024-05-06). They state for the `now/` directory: one archive per station, named `*_{product_code}_{station_id}_now.zip`; time stamps in UTC; CSV with delimiter `;`, line terminator CRLF and a header line; quality level `QN` with `1` = only formal control, `2` = controlled with individually defined criteria, `3` = automatic control and correction; missing value `-999`; "In the now/ directory, the data is updated at a frequency of < 1 h"; quality control is not completed for `now` data, so values may change; QN = 1 data "may contain significant errors". Licence: "The Creative Commons BY 4.0 - Licence "CC BY 4.0" apply".

| Product | Column | Description (DWD wording, abridged) | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `tu` | `PP_10` | air pressure at station altitude | hPa |
| `tu` | `TT_10` | air temperature 2 m above ground, instant | °C |
| `tu` | `TM5_10` | air temperature 5 cm above ground, instant | °C |
| `tu` | `RF_10` | relative humidity | % |
| `tu` | `TD_10` | dew point, calculated from `TT_10` and `RF_10` | °C |
| `ff` | `FF_10` | mean wind speed during the previous 10 minutes | m/s |
| `ff` | `DD_10` | mean wind direction during the previous 10 minutes | ° |
| `ff` | `SLA_10`, `SLO_10` | standard deviation of the lateral / longitudinal wind speed during the previous 10 minutes | none stated |
| `rr` | `RWS_10` | sum of the precipitation height of the previous 10 minutes | mm |
| `rr` | `RWS_DAU_10` | duration of precipitation | minutes |
| `rr` | `RWS_IND_10` | precipitation indicator (numerical code) | — |
| `sd` | `GS_10` | sum of global radiation during the previous 10 minutes | J/cm² |
| `sd` | `DS_10` | sum of diffuse sky radiation during the previous 10 minutes | J/cm² |
| `sd` | `LS_10` | sum of longwave radiation during the previous 10 minutes | J/cm² |
| `sd` | `SD_10` | sum of sunshine duration during the previous 10 minutes | h |

The descriptions do not state a statistic or period for `PP_10` and `RF_10`, and they document no calm-wind marker for `DD_10`. The precipitation description adds (section "CONSIDERATIONS FOR APPLICATIONS"): the meaning of the time stamp is instrument dependent; for the instrument rain[e]H3 it denotes the end of the measurement interval; for Pluvio Ott (DWD stations from November 2008) a delay of about 5 minutes applies, so a value stamped 11:50 UTC corresponds to the actual 10-minute interval ending 11:45 UTC; a precipitation indicator of 2 with a depth above 0 mm is a known uncorrected error. The `now` files name no instrument; the instruments are listed per station in the `meta_data/` archives (`Metadaten_Geraete*`), which P5 does not read. They give the `MESS_DATUM` format as `YYYYMMDDHH24`; the published `now` files use twelve digits (section 3.3).

### 3.3 `now` archives

- Archive names: `10minutenwerte_TU_<id>_now.zip`, `10minutenwerte_wind_<id>_now.zip`, `10minutenwerte_nieder_<id>_now.zip`, `10minutenwerte_SOLAR_<id>_now.zip` with the five-digit ID; counts `tu` 466, `ff` 277, `rr` 1371, `sd` 71.
- Every inspected archive (951 plus the four samples) has exactly one member `produkt_zehn_now_<code>_<yyyyMMdd>_<yyyyMMdd>_<id>.txt` whose ID matches the archive name; largest archive 1766 bytes, largest member 9568 bytes (both within the SC-19 bounds of 1 MiB), 48 to 132 data rows.
- Member text: ASCII, CRLF, final line terminated; header and columns exactly `STATIONS_ID;MESS_DATUM;  QN;PP_10;TT_10;TM5_10;RF_10;TD_10;eor` (`tu`), `STATIONS_ID;MESS_DATUM;  QN;FF_10;DD_10;eor` (`ff`), `STATIONS_ID;MESS_DATUM;  QN;RWS_DAU_10;RWS_10;RWS_IND_10;eor` (`rr`), `STATIONS_ID;MESS_DATUM;  QN;DS_10;GS_10;SD_10;LS_10;eor` (`sd`) in every inspected file. The wind `now` files carry no `SLA_10` or `SLO_10` column.
- `STATIONS_ID` is written without leading zeros and right-aligned (for example `        183`); 2 to 5 digits occur.
- `MESS_DATUM` always has twelve digits, `yyyyMMddHHmm` (for example `202610241830`), in UTC (description: "The time stamp is given in UTC"); rows are in ascending time order without duplicates; the interval is 10 minutes.
- Each file covers one UTC day: the first and last row lie on the same date. For 938 of the 951 bulk files that date is the check day (2026-10-24, rows from 00:00 up to 18:30 at the check time of 18:52); 13 files (`tu` 5, `ff` 1, `rr` 1, `sd` 6) hold an earlier day (back to 2026-08-01) and belong to stations that stopped reporting. The member name dates are `<begin>_<end>`: the begin date equals the date of the first row in every file; the end date equals it in 944 files and is the following day in 7 stale files (for example `produkt_zehn_now_ff_20260801_20260802_02961.txt` with rows from `202608010000` to `202608012050`; stations `01964`, `02961`, `06263`, `06272`). SC-19 checks only the name pattern, the product code and the station ID, not that the name dates match the rows, and P5 keeps it so. Directory times: current files were rewritten between 18:40 and 18:50 on the check day (listing fetched at 18:51); stale files keep their old times.
- `QN` values observed: `2` and `3` (no `1`); one `QN` per row for all its columns.
- Missing values: written as `-999` (no other spelling observed). Counts in the bulk set: `tu` `PP_10` 31539 (no valid value at 285 of 466 stations), `TT_10` 63, `TM5_10` 966, `RF_10` 114, `TD_10` 114; `ff` `FF_10` 8 and `DD_10` 8 (in the same rows); `rr` `RWS_10` 2, `RWS_DAU_10` and `RWS_IND_10` 12992 (no valid value at 117 of 137 inspected stations); `sd` `GS_10` 1341, `DS_10` 1452, `SD_10` 993, `LS_10` 4007.
- Value ranges observed (valid values): `TT_10` −11.5 to 21.4 °C; `RF_10` 33.4 to 100.0 %; `PP_10` 703.9 to 1016.3 hPa (station level, so mountain stations are far below 1000 hPa); `FF_10` 0.2 to 27.3 m/s; `DD_10` 10 to 360, always a multiple of 10, never 0; `RWS_10` 0.00 to 1.7 mm; `GS_10` 0.0 to 40.9 J/cm²; `SD_10` 0 to 0.167 h.
- Calm wind: none of the 30784 wind rows has `FF_10` = 0, so the snapshot shows no calm case; north is written as 360.
