# P7.09 – Export Equivalence and Schema Validation: Implementation Plan

**Status: executed on 2026-10-28 (reconciled with the approved [P7 forecast contract](../../p7/P7_FORECAST_CONTRACT.md) in P7.02 S6; [verification record](../../p7/P7_VERIFICATION.md) section 9).** Refines the P7.09 row of section 5 and the "Export" row of section 4 of [PHASE_P7_PLAN.md](../PHASE_P7_PLAN.md).

## 1. Goal and Scope
Goal: prove that the runs produced by both parsers serialize through the real JSON and XML serializers into equivalent, schema-valid documents that keep every forecast rule of the contract, without another retrieval.
In scope: runs of both products from the composed pipelines (only the HTTP handler mocked, synthetic KMZ files) through `System.Text.Json.JsonSerializer` and `System.Xml.Serialization.XmlSerializer` as wired by the export services; semantic equivalence of the two documents; validation against the regenerated v1 schema; UTC forms of `issued_at`, `retrieved_at`, `valid_at` and interval bounds; omitted values absent on both formats; zeros kept; leading-zero and alphanumeric MOSMIX IDs; umlauts and XML special characters in names; interval members with period and aggregation; the stale marker `run_age_exceeded` (`true` and `false`) and the forecast-only members `wind_gust_kmh` and `precipitation_amount_mm` of P7.03; `pressure_reference` `unknown`; S and L sources distinct; the `forecast_runs` collection of the envelope (C-83 to C-85); determinism for identical input.
Out of scope with owner: MQTT payloads → P8; CLI output files and stdout → P3.

## 2. Preconditions and Inputs
- P7.07 and P7.08 complete; P7.03 complete (schema regenerated where applicable).
- Existing: the export serializers and their contract tests, `ExportSchemaValidation`, `RealExportValidationTests`, `AllRootsDeterminismTests`, `ForecastRunExportEquivalenceTests`, the P5.08 and P6.08 export-equivalence tests as patterns.
